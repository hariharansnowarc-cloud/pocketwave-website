Delete your Pocketwave account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Pocketwave and tap your photo at the top-left.
  2. Tap Privacy.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Without the app

Email support@pocketwave.app from any address with the subject "Delete my Pocketwave account" and include your @username and the Google email or phone number you signed in with. We'll confirm it's you and delete the account within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, photo, interests and settings, and your sign-in email or phone number
  • Your messages, photos, voice notes and documents
  • Your connections, invites, group memberships and games
  • Your location data and notification tokens

Groups you own are handed to the longest-serving admin. Copies of messages someone reported may be kept as long as needed for safety reviews. Backups are cleared within 30 days.
