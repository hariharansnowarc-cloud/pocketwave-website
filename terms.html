2. How Pocketwave works

Nobody can message you until you accept. People can find you nearby only while you're Discoverable, or by your @username if you allow it. They can send an invite, and the chat opens only if you accept. Scanning each other's QR code connects you straight away, because showing your code counts as saying yes.

Being in the same group doesn't open a private chat. That still needs an invite and an accept.

If someone hasn't accepted your invite, leave it there. Don't keep inviting, don't follow them across groups, and don't make new accounts to get around a block.

4. Reporting and what we can see

You can report and block from any profile, chat, group member list or message. Blocking takes effect immediately.

Because chats are end-to-end encrypted, we can't read them. When you report a message, your phone sends us a copy of that message so we can look at it. We can also see profiles, group names, descriptions and photos. We act on reports with what we have. That's why your report matters.

Group admins can remove members and delete messages in their group. They're expected to run their groups within these rules.

7. Encryption and your keys

Your messages can only be unlocked with keys on your phone. That's what keeps them private, and it also means we can't recover them for you. If you lose your phone and haven't set a backup PIN, or you forget the PIN, older encrypted messages are gone for good. New messages will still work.

8. Location
