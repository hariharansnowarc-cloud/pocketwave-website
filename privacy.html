Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

Pocketwave ("we", "us") is a messaging app that lets you find people near you, but only while you choose to be visible, and only lets a chat start when both people agree. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Pocketwave is operated by Hari Haran, Sankarankovil, Tamil Nadu, India.

In short: we never show your location, distance, email or phone number to other users. Your location is stored only while you are Discoverable and is deleted when that ends. We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.

1. Information we collect

WhatWhy
Sign-in details: your Google account's name, email address and profile picture link (if you continue with Google), or your phone number (if you sign in with a code)To create your account and sign you in. Your email and phone number are never shown to other users. We only ask Google for your basic profile and email, never your contacts, files or other Google data.
Profile: first and last name, @username, photo, status, bio, interests; optionally birth year and genderSo people can recognise you. Age and gender are hidden unless you switch them on.
Location (only while Discoverable is on, or when a group admin sets a group's location)To show you people and groups within the discovery radius. Rounded to about 11 m, used only on our server, never shown to anyone, and deleted when Discoverable ends.
Messages and media: text, photos, voice notes, documents, reactions, repliesTo deliver your chats. Media is stored privately and only members of that chat can open it.
Connections and activity: invites, groups, blocks, games, read receipts, last seen (if you turn it on)To run the app's features.
ReportsWhen you report a message or person, a copy of the reported message is kept so we can review it.
Device and app data: push notification token, phone model, operating system version, app versionTo send you notifications, and to understand problems on specific phones.
Crash and error reports: the error message, the screen where it happened, app version and phone modelTo find and fix bugs. Kept for 30 days. They don't include your messages or location.
Feedback you send usTo improve the app. Sent with your name, app version and phone model.

We do not collect your contacts, and we do not track your location in the background.

2. Permissions on your phone

Location (while using the app, only when you go Discoverable or set a group location) · Camera (scan QR codes, take photos) · Photos / media (send and save photos) · Microphone (voice notes) · Notifications. You can turn any of these off in your phone's settings.

3. Who can see what

  • Before you connect: others can see your first name, photo, status, bio and interests (age and gender only if you switch them on).
  • After you connect: also your last name and @username, and last seen if you both turn it on.
  • Nobody ever sees your email, phone number, exact location or distance.
  • Link previews are created on the sender's phone, so the people you message never contact the linked website.

4. Service providers

We use trusted providers to run the app. They process data only on our instructions:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage (servers in Mumbai, India).
  • Google: if you choose "Continue with Google", Google confirms your identity and shares your name, email and profile picture link with us.
  • SMS provider: to send sign-in codes, if you sign in with a phone number.
  • Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: to deliver notifications (the notification shows the sender's name and a message preview).

We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers. We may disclose information if required by Indian law or a valid legal order.

5. How long we keep data

  • Location: deleted when Discoverable ends (checked every 5 minutes).
  • Messages and media: kept until you or the sender delete them, the chat or group is deleted, or you delete your account.
  • Reports: kept as long as needed to keep the community safe.
  • When you delete your account we delete your profile, photos, files, messages and memberships. Backups are cleared within 30 days.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and protected by strict access rules on our database, so only the right people can read each chat. Messages are not yet end-to-end encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't share very sensitive information.

7. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can access, correct and delete your personal data, withdraw consent, and raise a complaint. Most of this you can do in the app (Edit profile, Privacy settings, Delete account). For anything else, email us. If you're not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Age

Pocketwave is only for people aged 18 or over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger; if we learn of it, we delete the account.

9. Deleting your account

In the app: Profile & settings → Privacy → Delete account. Or see pocketwave.app/delete-account.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
