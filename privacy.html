Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

Pocketwave is a small app made in Sankarankovil, Tamil Nadu. It helps you meet people who are around you, at a fest, a concert or a mall, and then chat with them. We built it around one idea: nobody should be able to reach you unless you say yes, and nobody should be able to tell where you are.

This page explains, without the legal fog, what we collect, what we do with it and what we never do. If something here is unclear, write to us. A real person reads every email.

The short version
  • Your chats are end-to-end encrypted. We store them, but we can't read them.
  • Other people never see your location, your distance from them, or your email.
  • We keep your location only while Discoverable is on. When it switches off, the location is deleted.
  • No ads. We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't share it with advertisers.

1. What we collect, and why

When you sign up

If you continue with Google, Google tells us your name, email address and a link to your profile picture. We use these only to create your account and sign you in. Nobody else on Pocketwave can see them.

You then pick a username and add a first name, a photo and, if you like, a last name, status, short bio and up to five interests. Birth year and gender are optional and stay hidden unless you switch them on.

Your location

We only ask for location when you turn on Discoverable, or when a group admin taps "Set location to here". The app never tracks you in the background.

While you're Discoverable, your phone sends its position every couple of minutes. We round it to about 11 metres and use it on our server for one thing: working out who else is discoverable within the distance the app allows (200 m by default). Other people only see that you are nearby. There's no map, no distance and no "closest first" order, so nobody can work out where you are standing. When your Discoverable time runs out or you switch it off, the location is deleted. A clean-up job also clears anything expired every five minutes.

A group's location is set by its admins and stays with the group until they change it. It is only used while the group is shown in Nearby.

Your messages

Messages, photos, voice notes, documents, captions, file names and link previews are end-to-end encrypted. They are locked on the sender's phone and can only be unlocked on the phones of the people in that chat. Our servers hold the locked copies so they can be delivered, but we don't have the keys.

What we can see is the information needed to run the service: who is in a chat, when a message was sent, what type it is (text, photo, voice or document), file sizes, photo dimensions, voice note length, reactions, and delivery and read ticks. Messages sent before encryption was switched on (November 2026) stay as they were.

Link previews are made on the sender's phone, so the website sees the sender's phone visiting it. The person receiving the message never contacts that website.

Encryption keys and your backup PIN

Each account has a key pair. The private half stays in your phone's secure storage. The public half is stored on our server so others can send you encrypted messages.

If you set a backup PIN, your phone locks a copy of your private key with that PIN and stores the locked copy with us, so you can read your chats on a new phone. We never receive the PIN itself. After five wrong tries, the backup is locked for an hour. If you forget the PIN, we can't open the backup for you.

Connections, groups and games

We store invites, connections, blocks, group memberships and roles, join requests, and games (moves, scores and results). Games are not end-to-end encrypted. Invites expire after seven days. If someone declines you, they aren't shown again for 30 days, and we never tell you that they declined.

Notifications

To send notifications we keep a push token for your phone. Encrypted messages travel to your phone still locked, and your phone unlocks them to show the text. If you'd rather not see message text on your lock screen, turn off Show message text in notifications in Privacy settings. Notifications pass through Expo's push service and Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging.

Reports, feedback and crash reports

When you report a message, your phone sends us a copy of that one message (we can't read it any other way), along with the reason you chose. When you send feedback from the app, it comes with your name, app version and phone model. If the app crashes, it sends us the error, the screen it happened on, the app version and the phone model. Crash reports never include your messages or location, and we delete them after 30 days.

What we don't collect

Your contacts or address book. Your location in the background. Advertising IDs. Anything from other apps on your phone. The website pocketwave.app doesn't use cookies or analytics.

3. How long we keep things

  • Location: until Discoverable ends (it's checked every five minutes).
  • Messages and files: until you or the sender delete them, the chat or group is deleted, or the account is deleted. "Delete for everyone" removes the content from our server.
  • Your account: until you delete it. Deleting it removes your profile, photos, the messages and files you sent, your keys and backup, and your memberships. Messages other people sent you stay on their phones.
  • Reports: as long as we need them to deal with the problem and keep people safe.
  • Crash reports: 30 days.
  • Database backups: overwritten within 30 days.

4. Your choices and rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, have it erased, and withdraw your consent. You can also nominate someone to act for you if you die or can't act yourself. Most of this you can do in the app:

  • Edit profile: change or remove your details and photo.
  • Privacy settings: read receipts, last seen, whether people can find you by @username, message text in notifications, your backup PIN.
  • Discoverable: switch it off at any time; your location is deleted.
  • Delete account: Profile → Privacy → Delete account, or see pocketwave.app/delete-account.

For anything else, email the grievance officer below. If you aren't happy with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

5. Age

Pocketwave is for people aged 18 and over. Indian law needs a parent's verified consent to process a child's data, and the app isn't designed for children, so we don't allow under-18s. If we find an account that belongs to someone younger, we delete it.

6. Security

Besides end-to-end encryption, everything travels over HTTPS, and our database only lets each person reach their own data and the chats they belong to. Even so, no app is perfectly secure. If we ever have a breach that affects you, we'll tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

7. Changes

When this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If the change matters (for example, a new kind of data or a new company handling it), we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
