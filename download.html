Download for Android

Only download Pocketwave from this page.

How to install

  1. Tap Download for Android. If Chrome warns that the file might be harmful, tap Download anyway. It shows that for every app that isn't from the Play Store.
  2. Open the downloaded file (from the notification, or Files → Downloads).
  3. The first time, Android asks to allow installs from Chrome. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.

Updating

When there's a new version, the app tells you. Download it from this page and install it over the old one. Your account and chats stay.

Found a bug?

In the app: tap your photo → Send feedback. Or email support@pocketwave.app.
