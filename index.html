How it works

No endless swiping, no strangers in your inbox. Just the people who are actually here.

1

Turn on Discoverable

Pick one, three or six hours. When the time's up, you disappear on your own.

2

See who's around

Only people nearby who've switched it on too. No maps, no distances.

3

Say hi

Send an invite. If they accept, you chat. Or scan each other's QR code and connect on the spot.

Private by design

Meeting strangers only works if you feel safe doing it, so we built Pocketwave around that.

  • Nobody can message you until you accept
  • Your location, distance and phone number are never shown
  • Your location is deleted as soon as Discoverable ends
  • If you decline someone, they're never told
  • No ads, and we don't sell your data
🔒

End-to-end encrypted

Messages, photos, voice notes and files are locked on your phone and only unlock on the phones of the people in the chat. We can't read them, and nobody who gets into our servers can either.

Running an event?

Fests, concerts, conferences and college events can use Pocketwave to help people meet. Create a group, show it to everyone at the venue, and approve who joins. Get in touch.

Questions

Is Pocketwave free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription.

Can people see where I am?

No. Others only see that you're nearby, never a map, a distance or your exact spot. Your location is only used while Discoverable is on, then deleted.

Who can message me?

Only people whose invite you've accepted, or people whose QR code you've scanned. Being in the same group doesn't open a private chat.

What if I change phones?

Set a backup PIN in Privacy settings. On your new phone, sign in and enter the PIN to get your chats back.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. Pocketwave is on Android for now.

Who can use it?

Anyone aged 18 or over.
